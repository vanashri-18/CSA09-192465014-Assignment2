# Task 1 - Experimental Results

## Workload 1 - Small
Tickets: 1
Fields per ticket: 6

- String: 0.03 ms
- StringBuilder: 0.01 ms
- StringBuffer: 0.01 ms

## Workload 2 - Medium
Tickets: 1000
Fields per ticket: 10

- String: 6.96 ms
- StringBuilder: 3.05 ms
- StringBuffer: 2.44 ms

## Workload 3 - Large
Tickets: 10000
Fields per ticket: 50

- String: 49.61 ms
- StringBuilder: 20.19 ms
- StringBuffer: 26.20 ms

## Preliminary Observation
StringBuilder performed fastest in the large workload. String was slowest in all three workloads in this particular experiment. More repeated measurements are needed for a reliable conclusion.
