## Memory Information
Memory before: 502 KB

## Observation
StringBuilder performed fastest in the large workload. String was slowest in all three workloads in this experiment. StringBuffer was slower than StringBuilder in the large workload.

Note: Memory values are approximate heap measurements before and after the complete experiment. They are not method-specific memory measurements.
