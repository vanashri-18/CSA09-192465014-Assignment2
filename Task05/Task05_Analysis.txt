## 10. Faulty Comparator Test

A second test was performed using a faulty comparator that compares only team points.

Test data:
- Zebras (12 points, goal difference 2)
- Aces (12 points, goal difference 8)
- Bears (12 points, goal difference 8)

Faulty output:
[Zebras(12,2), Aces(12,8), Bears(12,8)]

Correct output:
[Aces(12,8), Bears(12,8), Zebras(12,2)]

The faulty comparator ignores goal difference and team name. The correct comparator considers all three ranking rules and produces the expected order.
